OPM UYO TOUCHING LIVES

OPM UYO TOUCHING LIVES is a humanitarian and skills-acquisition initiative focused on practical empowerment. The program has conducted activities for five years and counting and has trained more than 1,000 people.

Our next phase is to strengthen partnerships and expand mobile training so that more communities can access practical skills.

Our Vision

A future where more people can access practical skills, build livelihoods and contribute confidently to their communities.
