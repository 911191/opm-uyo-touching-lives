About OPM UYO TOUCHING LIVES

OPM UYO TOUCHING LIVES is a humanitarian and skills-empowerment initiative focused on practical learning, service, opportunity creation and community development.

Through practical training and community outreach, the initiative seeks to equip people with useful skills that can create confidence, independence and opportunities.
