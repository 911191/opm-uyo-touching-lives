News & Events

Create updates with images, YouTube Shorts, YouTube videos, Facebook videos/reels, direct MP4/WebM/OGG URLs, or upload a video from your phone.

Media supported

  • YouTube: normal watch links, youtu.be links, Shorts links and embed links.
  • Facebook: public video/reel links and supported Facebook video embed links.
  • Direct video: MP4, WebM or OGG URLs.
  • Phone upload: video files up to 50 MB, uploaded directly to the configured media host.
  • Playback: videos are muted/autoplay where the browser permits, with native controls and fullscreen support.
⚙ Media Upload Settings

For phone uploads, use a Cloudinary unsigned upload preset restricted to video files with a 50 MB maximum. These two values are saved only in this administrator browser; no Cloudinary secret is stored here.
