Training Applications

News & Events Management

Create, edit, publish and remove news, announcements, events and video updates.

Media: Add an image URL and/or a public video URL. Video links can be direct MP4/WebM/OGG files or supported YouTube links. No new Vercel Function is required.

Gallery Management

Manage training, humanitarian and community photographs shown in the public gallery.

Gallery control: use Active to publish or hide an item and Sort order to control its position. ImgBB links are supported.

Homepage Special Management

Select and order the featured photos displayed on the homepage.

Homepage rule: active specials are shown first. Sort order controls their display order; the homepage keeps the existing design and falls back to the gallery when no special is selected.
