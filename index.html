About us

Touching Lives Through Practical Empowerment

OPM UYO TOUCHING LIVES is a humanitarian and skills-empowerment initiative focused on practical learning, service, opportunity creation and community development.

Our focus

We believe practical skills can open doors to independence, confidence and opportunity.

  • Skills acquisition
  • Community empowerment
  • Mobile training opportunities
  • Partnership and CSR collaboration
  • Volunteer and community support
